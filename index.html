I am a Ph.D. student at NTU CSIE, advised by Prof. Yun-Nung (Vivian) Chen at the MiuLab. My research focuses on building conversational agents that remember, adapt, and act: agent memory systems, language model personalization, and conversational agents & dialogue systems. I joined the Ph.D. program directly from NTU's M.S. program in Data Science, after a B.B.A. in Accounting at NTU, and spent nearly three years building LLM-based agentic systems in industry.
